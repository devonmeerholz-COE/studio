# Welcome to Super Chilled Studio

Super Chilled Studio creates meaningful, atmospheric places that people want to return to.

## The Constitution

[PROJECT.md](PROJECT.md) is the project's constitution. Read it before beginning any work, without exception. It defines the Studio's mission, philosophy, worlds, and laws.

The Studio Laws are engineering requirements, not suggestions. Every implementation must protect the fixed camera, the living world, the Bonsai, the Beacon, purposeful objects, calm movement, and continuity across the five worlds.

Creative intent always overrides automation. Automation exists to remove repetition and support craftsmanship; when it conflicts with the Studio's atmosphere, identity, continuity, or human judgment, change or stop the automation.
