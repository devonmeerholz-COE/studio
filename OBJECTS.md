# SUPER CHILLED STUDIO

# OBJECTS

Version: 1.0

> A life is remembered through the things we choose to keep.

---

# Purpose

This document defines recurring objects that carry identity, continuity, or emotional meaning across Super Chilled Studio.

It is not a catalogue of props.

Objects behave like recurring characters.

They are remembered before they are noticed.

Their meaning grows through repetition, continuity, and quiet familiarity.

The Studio tells its story through the things the resident chooses to keep.

Objects reveal identity, history, continuity, and emotional meaning through presence, wear, repair, and age.

Recognition Level describes how quickly a returning viewer should notice an object. It is an art-direction field, not a measure of importance.

---

## The Protagonist’s Bonsai

**Canonical name:** The Protagonist’s Bonsai

**Canonical ID:** `protagonist-bonsai`

**Category:** Canon

**Recognition Level:** Familiar

**First appearance:** Midnight Library

**Purpose:**

Represents quiet growth across an entire lifetime.

It reminds the viewer that meaningful change is measured in years rather than moments.

**Studio rules:** It remains present throughout its established continuity. It may age and grow naturally. In the Hacker Apartment, it is also the Beacon.

**Evolution:**

- **Midnight Library** — earliest life stage
- **Hacker Apartment** — young and also the Beacon
- **Rainy Loft** — mature and larger
- **Space Station** — old, larger, and carefully sustained

**Continuity notes:** This is the same protagonist-owned Bonsai across the Midnight Library, Hacker Apartment, Rainy Loft, and Space Station. It is distinct from the Coffee Café house Bonsai.

**Appearances:**

- **Midnight Library** — earliest life stage
- **Hacker Apartment** — young and also the Beacon
- **Rainy Loft** — mature
- **Space Station** — old and carefully sustained

# Guiding Principle

Objects are remembered through repetition.

An object that quietly returns over decades carries more emotional weight than a new object introduced for a single moment.

When uncertain, allow an existing object to continue its journey rather than creating another.
