# Forge

> The production engine behind Super Chilled Studio.

---

## Mission

Forge exists to help create meaningful places that people want to return to.

Its purpose is not simply to automate YouTube production.

Its purpose is to protect the philosophy, identity and craftsmanship of Super Chilled Studio while removing repetitive work from the creative process.

---

# Core Philosophy

We create places, not videos.

We create atmosphere, not content.

We create consistency, not novelty.

We create hope.

Every decision made by Forge should reinforce these principles.

---

# Studio Laws

## The Camera Law

The camera never moves.

The world moves.

---

## The Bonsai Law

Every world contains the Bonsai.

It quietly grows throughout the series.

---

## The Beacon Law

Every world contains one permanent object that tells returning visitors they are home.

---

## The Purpose Law

Every object exists for a reason.

Nothing is placed randomly.

---

## The Calm Law

Movement should never distract.

Animation exists only to make the world feel alive.

---

# The Five Worlds

1. Hacker Apartment
