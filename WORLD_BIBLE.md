# SUPER CHILLED STUDIO

# WORLD BIBLE

Version: 1.0

Status: CANON

---

# Purpose

Super Chilled Studio does not create videos.

It creates meaningful places people want to return to.

Every world exists to improve someone's day, even if only a little.

The viewer is never an observer.

The viewer lives here.

---

# The Protagonist

The viewer is the protagonist.

No face is ever shown.

No voice is ever heard.

The audience experiences each world as though it belongs to them.

As they grow older...

...the worlds grow with them.

---

# Core Philosophy

We create places.

Not content.

We create atmosphere.

Not distraction.

We create consistency.

Not novelty.

We create hope.

Always.

---

# The Seven Studio Laws

## Law I — The Witness

The world moves.

The camera witnesses.

The camera never pans, zooms, tilts or cuts.

Everything that feels alive happens naturally inside the world.

The viewer should feel present, never directed.

---

## Law II — Purpose Before Decoration

Nothing exists simply because it looks good.

Every object has a reason to exist.

Every room tells the story of the person who lives there.

If an object cannot justify its place, it does not belong.

---

## Law III — Living Worlds

The worlds evolve.

The viewer notices.

Technology improves.

Objects age.

Books accumulate.

Plants grow.

Weather changes.

Life leaves evidence.

Nothing resets between episodes.

---

## Law IV — The Bonsai

The Bonsai is home.

It appears in every world.

It grows slowly over the decades.

Its growth quietly tells the passage of time.

It is the Studio's living logo.

---

## Law V — The Beacon

Every world contains one permanent object that never meaningfully changes.

The Beacon tells returning visitors:

"You've come home."

The Beacon is unique to each world.

---

## Law VI — Quiet Motion

Movement creates atmosphere.

Movement never demands attention.

Rain falls.

Steam rises.

Lights flicker.

Leaves drift.

Screens glow.

Nothing moves merely to entertain.

---

## Law VII — Hope

Every world must leave the viewer feeling slightly better than when they arrived.

Hope is not spoken.

Hope is felt.

Everything the Studio creates ultimately serves this purpose.
